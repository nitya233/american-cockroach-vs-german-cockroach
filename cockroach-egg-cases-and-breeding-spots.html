Cockroach egg cases and breeding spots

Cockroach egg cases and breeding spots are easy to overlook because they are often not in open view. For homeowners, the priority is to check likely hiding and entry points rather than only treating visible insects. Cabinet hinges, cracks, appliance gaps, wall voids, skirting, entry points and drain perimeters are areas to inspect. Because treatment is chosen by species, identifying the cockroach and its harbourage helps decide whether gel baiting, residual insecticide, insecticidal dust or granular bait is appropriate.
