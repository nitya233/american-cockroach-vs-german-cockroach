American cockroach vs German cockroach

For homeowners, the practical question is not just which cockroach is seen but which species is present. American and German cockroaches are different species, and the method is chosen by species. This applies in HDB flats, condominiums, landed homes, offices, restaurants and commercial kitchens. Inspection of high-risk areas comes first. Gel baiting, residual insecticide, insecticidal dust and granular bait may be used depending on species and harbourage. Accurate identification helps avoid treating the wrong areas.

For a homeowner, the useful comparison is what activity looks like and where treatment should focus. Check cabinet hinges, cracks, appliance gaps, skirting, entry points, drain perimeters and wall voids rather than only open floor areas. Seeing one cockroach does not confirm the species or the source. Because the method is chosen by species, American and German cockroaches should be identified before gel baiting, residual insecticide, insecticidal dust or granular bait is selected. Treating visible insects without addressing hiding and entry points can leave activity returning.

Common questions

How can I tell if I have American or German cockroaches?

Species should be confirmed by inspection rather than by a single sighting. The method is chosen by species. Note activity in cabinet hinges, cracks, appliance gaps, skirting, entry points, drain perimeters or wall voids so the inspection can focus on likely hiding and entry points.

Does treatment differ by species?

Yes. The method is chosen by species and may include gel baiting, residual insecticide, insecticidal dust or granular bait. American and German cockroaches are both covered, but the inspection should identify the species and the hiding and entry points before treatment is selected.
